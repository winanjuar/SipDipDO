# Adversarial Review v3 — Architecture Spine (snd-dash, 2026-09-15) — re-gate after fixes

**Verdict: HOLES-FOUND**

All 14 prior pairs (P1–P14) are **genuinely closed** by the amendments — each fix eliminates the divergence mechanism, not merely renames it (verification per pair below). But attacking the AMENDED text produced **7 new findings: 3 high, 2 medium, 2 low**. Two of the highs are wounds opened by the fixes themselves (the AD-7 price-correction mechanism retroactively mutates Harga Terkunci; the migration "full finalization door" enumeration contradicts AD-2's door contents). One high is a missed writer adjacent to the P14 fix (finalized cut-off period contents are not frozen). None requires paradigm change; all are closeable with the proposed AD text.

## Part 2 — New findings (constructed divergent pairs)

### High — 3

**N1 (High) — AD-7's correction mechanism mutates Harga Terkunci retroactively: FK-snapshot vs value-snapshot.**
- **Unit A** (pricing CMS, correction story) corrects today's price 52.000 → 51.000 by **updating the running `price_periods` row** (**compliant**: AD-7 verbatim — "koreksi harga pada tanggal efektif yang sama = mengubah baris berjalan + audit, bukan menambah baris kedua").
- **Unit B** (orders submit + ledger finalization stories) models Harga Terkunci as the FK shown in the ERD (`buy_orders }o--|| price_periods : "snapshot Harga Terkunci"`, `ledger_transactions }o--|| price_periods : "harga final"`) with no copied value column (**compliant**: the ERD is the seed; nothing in AD-7/AD-2 requires storing the value).
- **Collision:** an order submitted this morning at 52.000 (still pending) silently becomes a 51.000 order — confirm re-validates and finalizes at the mutated value; FR-1/FR-20's "Harga final tetap Harga Terkunci saat submit meskipun harga berjalan berubah" is broken by the spine's own correction rule. Worse for closed transactions: a same-date correction after confirmation mutates the ledger row's price via FK → Bukti regenerated "pada titik potong transaksinya" (State convention) reads the **current** row value and shows a price the owner never paid → tampering suspicion, exactly the SM-1/SM-3 wound P4 was fixed to prevent. Note AD-1 forbids UPDATE only on `ledger_transactions`, not on `price_periods` — the mutation path is legal.
- **Close it (AD-7 + konvensi Data & format):** "Harga Terkunci disimpan sebagai **nilai** pada `buy_orders` dan **harga final sebagai nilai** pada `ledger_transactions` (FK hanya menunjuk baris asal); mengubah baris `price_periods` (koreksi) tidak pernah mengubah nilai yang telah ter-snapshot atau ter-final — Bukti dan re-validasi selalu memakai nilai tersimpan, bukan isi mutakhir baris harga."

**N2 (High) — Migration "pintu finalisasi lengkap" contradicts AD-2's door contents: plotting/pembayaran/gerbang untuk baris historis tidak dipinkan.**
- **Unit A** (migration importer story) reads "pintu finalisasi lengkap" as AD-2's full door: appends ledger + **plotting alokasi ke Capital Item** per item from the Sheets' Pemenuhan column + payment fields + re-validating Strength and the aggregate adjustment bound on each historical row (**compliant**: AD-2 defines the finalization tx as containing exactly these; the migration convention says "lengkap").
- **Unit B** (migration importer story, different build) follows the migration convention's own enumeration — "posisi, event Pembelian Pertama efektif, audit aktor `system`" — which **omits plotting, pencatatan pembayaran, and penyesuaian instant**, and skips domain-gate re-validation since FR-14 makes the Sheets authoritative and the acceptance gate is only the Grand Total (Quantity 3.622 / Shares 5.187 / Ceil 14.980) (**compliant**: it implements exactly the enumerated list; nothing forbids omitting the un-enumerated parts).
- **Collision:** Fulfillment is *derived from plotting* (AD-2). Under B, historical plotting is empty → Fulfillment Rate 0% → the Modal Tetap/Bergerak purchase gates (FR-23) read as **open** post-go-live despite a full RKAP → owners order into a closed phase, confirm-time re-validation reads the same empty Fulfillment and passes → real money over-collected against a 100%-filled RKAP, repairable only via compensation entries. Under A, any historical row that violates Strength ≤ 100% or the 1%+1-saham bound (possible for real legacy data — e.g. an owner loaded on Modal Operasional beyond RTL) **blocks the entire migration**, violating FR-14's go-live acceptance. Both builds pass every AD sentence; they build opposite systems.
- **Close it (konvensi Migrasi):** "Pintu finalisasi migrasi = AD-2 lengkap **termasuk plotting alokasi per Capital Item** (direkonstruksi dari kolom Pemenuhan spreadsheet; acceptance mencakup paritas Fulfillment Rate per jenis modal, bukan hanya Grand Total) dan **pencatatan pembayaran historis** (metode `'migrasi'` bila tak diketahui); NAMUN **tanpa re-validasi gerbang domain** (Strength, ruang RKAP, batas penyesuaian) dan **tanpa penyesuaian instant** atas baris historis — data spreadsheet bersifat otoritatif; gerbang berlaku untuk transaksi baru pasca go-live saja."

**N3 (High) — Finalized cut-off period contents are not frozen: snapshot-at-cut-off vs derived-at-rekap.**
- **Unit A** (cut-off story, CONTRIBUTION) freezes an immutable per-owner points snapshot when the period is finalized; the recap story it assumes reads those snapshots (**compliant**: FR-10 "difinalkan … ditandai selesai"; nothing contradicts freezing).
- **Unit B** (rekap story, DISTRIBUSI) computes each finalized period's totals by summing `contribution_entries` at recap time — entries carry `period_id` and AD-10 only pins *which* periods are summed, not that their contents are immutable (**compliant**: "poin periode yang telah difinalkan cut-off" derived from entries is an equally valid reading; no AD makes contribution entries append-only or freezes them at cut-off — AD-1's append-only covers only `ledger_transactions`).
- **The vehicle:** the contribution-entry writer (a COO realisasi story) is absent from every freeze rule — nothing forbids recording a backdated entry (tanggal ≤ cut-off) or editing an existing entry **after** the period is finalized but **before** RUPS tunes it. That is precisely the missed-writer case the global lock ladder does not cover (locks order access; they do not freeze content).
- **Collision:** with any late/backdated/corrected entry in a finalized-untuned period, A's denominator ≠ B's denominator → the Insentif pool divides over different total points → owners paid wrong amounts, frozen into the immutable RUPS recap (AD-10) — SM-1 broken after the fact with no compensation path except another RUPS.
- **Close it (AD-5/AD-10 + konvensi State):** "Cut-off membekukan **snapshot poin sah per owner per periode (imutabel)** — rekap hanya membaca snapshot terbeku, tidak pernah re-derive dari entries. `contribution_entries` pada periode ter-finalisasi beku: koreksi/backdating pasca cut-off hanya masuk periode berikut sebagai penyesuaian carry-over; writer entries wajib menolak menulis ke periode berstatus final."

### Medium — 2

**N4 (Medium) — OTP failed-attempt counter cannot survive the aborted verification transaction (and single-live-code on resend unpinned).**
- **Unit A** (IDENTITAS OTP stories) records failed verification attempts in its **own committed write** (separate from the finalization tx), enforcing the attempt limit across tries (**compliant**: AD-8 says an attempt-limit exists and is "dienforce server-side").
- **Unit B** (finalization endpoint story) increments the attempt counter **inside the verification tx** — which aborts on wrong code, rolling the increment back with it (**compliant**: AD-8 mandates verify+consume "DI DALAM transaksi finalisasi" and B implements exactly that; the AD never states where the counter lives or that it must persist across aborted verification txs — the sentence even places the attempt-limit "pada jalur `requestOtp`", the wrong path).
- **Collision:** in B the limit can never trigger → unlimited 6-digit guessing against the finalization endpoint within the 10-minute TTL (resend-cooldown throttles emails, not guesses); in A the limit holds. Same spec, opposite security properties. Adjacent ambiguity: resend — invalidate the previous live code or leave multiple live rows? Unpinned; B-with-multiple-live-rows also defeats the dialog's "kode kedaluwarsa" state.
- **Close it (AD-8):** "Penghitung percobaan gagal OTP ditulis dalam transaksi **terpisah yang selalu commit** (di luar tx finalisasi) oleh IDENTITAS saat verifikasi gagal; batas percobaan dicek sebelum verifikasi. `requestOtp` baru **meng-invalideasi semua kode hidup sebelumnya** untuk `(action_type, target_ref)` tersebut — paling banyak satu baris OTP hidup per aksi."

**N5 (Medium) — Compensation entry (AD-1) has no door, no MFA classification, no validation semantics.**
- **Unit A** (COO correction story) implements compensation via the input-langsung door with MFA and full re-validation — including rejecting a compensation that would push Strength > 100% (**compliant**: AD-1 allows "hanya melalui dua pintu finalisasi" + MFA closed set covers input langsung).
- **Unit B** (COO correction story, different build) implements compensation as a direct LEDGER internal service — a de-facto third door — without MFA and without domain gates ("a correction must always be recordable"; the MFA closed set in AD-8 does not list compensation, and AD-1's door enumeration names only konfirmasi/input-langsung/migrasi, none of which a correction is) (**compliant** against the letter of both ADs).
- **Collision:** the same correction request succeeds in B and is rejected (or MFA-challenged) in A; B's unvalidated compensation can create a Strength > 100% position that then blocks the owner's next legitimate order under the AD-6 gate, or overfills RKAP space without bound re-validation. The two builds also disagree on whether a third mutation door into `positions` exists at all — the exact class of hole AD-1 was written to prevent.
- **Close it (AD-1 + AD-8):** "Entry kompensasi adalah varian pintu input langsung: MFA (FR-21), pencatatan pembayaran koreksi, audit dengan referensi baris asal, dan **tanpa re-validasi gerbang domain** (kompensasi justru boleh memperbaiki state yang melanggar gerbang — mis. menurunkan Strength); pintu ini di-enforce lewat API internal LEDGER yang sama; tidak ada jalur tulis posisi keempat."

### Low — 2

**N6 (Low) — Keluar-flip eligibility for zero-share owners with zero untuned points.**
- **Unit A** (rekap story) flips **every** owner re-validated `shares = 0` at the recap, reading the Glossary "setelah itu dinyatakan Keluar" broadly. **Unit B** flips only owners whose Insentif was actually ditunaikan (`> 0`), reading AD-11's trigger event literally ("`Insentif owner tanpa saham ditunaikan di rekap RUPS` → status Keluar"). Both compliant; AD-11 pins the re-validation (`shares = 0`) but not the candidate set. **Collision:** an owner zeroed by a compensation entry who never earned points stays full-access forever under B, becomes Keluar under A — divergent access matrices. **Close it (AD-11):** "Kandidat flip = seluruh owner dengan `positions.shares = 0` pada transaksi rekap (dengan atau tanpa insentif ditunaikan) — kepemilikan berakhir saat saham nol dan tidak ada poin belum-tunaikan."

**N7 (Low) — Re-registration after `kedaluwarsa`: same-row CAS or new owner row.**
- **Unit A** (pendaftaran story) re-registers by CAS `kedaluwarsa → diajukan` on the same owners row ("re-daftar diperbolehkan", AD-11). **Unit B** (same story, different build) inserts a fresh owners row for the returning Google account — the legal transition edges of the pinned enum don't include `kedaluwarsa → diajukan` explicitly, and nothing pins email uniqueness. **Collision:** duplicate accounts colliding on email-matched login vs clean re-activation; audit trail shows different lifecycles. **Close it (AD-11 + konvensi Data & format):** "Owners unik per email (unique constraint); re-daftar = CAS `kedaluwarsa → diajukan` pada baris yang sama (edge transisi legal, tercatat audit); tidak pernah baris owner baru untuk email yang sama."

## Checked and found sound (no finding)

- **Global ladder topology:** acyclic across all enumerated writers; price_periods / coo_tenures / otp_codes / audit writers are single-resource (no cycle possible); submit/withdraw/confirm/expiry all enter at `buy_orders`; rekap enters at `positions` per AD-11 — no inversion survives.
- **Canonical pending vs 4-state enum:** consistent — withdrawn orders leave the Antrian/lists per EXPERIENCE and are excluded from every guard by the canonical predicate; the enum stays PRD-faithful (4 states).
- **OTP in-tx vs UX dialog/TTL:** the dialog's "input → verifikasi → aksi berlanjut" is implementable as the single finalization submit; TTL 10 menit and cooldown 60s are consistent with AD-8; "Esc membatalkan seluruh aksi" holds since nothing commits before submit (residual N4 aside).
- **Pendaftaran enum vs FR-13 / Phase-2 bekas-pemegang:** `terverifikasi` + `first_effective_at` + the rekap flip reproduces all three FR-13 statuses including the Phase-2 bekas-pemegang (full access while untuned points remain, enforced via the flip, not via live shares) — consistent.
- **Bukti cut-off × migration no-outbox:** regenerate-on-demand at the cut point replays the migrated ledger; no interaction hole (price-mutation caveat handled in N1).
- **Freshness contract × PWA prompt rules:** refetch-on-visibility is not a reload and never occludes a dialog; the SW update prompt ban is orthogonal — no conflict.
